Two groups, with a gap of nearly four tenths and nothing in it:

    in corpus       0.2442  to  0.4192
    out of corpus   0.8026  to  0.9753

CUTOFF: 0.6 -- the starter default, kept rather than changed. Any number
between about 0.45 and 0.80 behaves identically on these ten questions, so
0.6 sits near the middle of the gap with room on both sides.

What both directions would cost:
    too low  (0.3)   refuses all five questions it had the answer to
    too high (0.9)   never refuses anything, including the World Cup

The gap is this wide because the out-of-scope questions come from a
completely different world: engines, football, Rust. A question about a
nearby town these guides don't happen to cover would land much closer to the
line, and that has not been tested.
