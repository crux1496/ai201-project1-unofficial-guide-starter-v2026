======================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES
======================================================================

Characterises the system as a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline rather
than describing it procedurally: corpus composition (14 guides, ~29.5 KB of
markdown, 96 indexed chunks), the embedding and retrieval configuration
(all-MiniLM-L6-v2 under cosine distance, k=5, Chroma), and the generation
step's constraint to the retrieved evidence.

States the query class the system targets -- factual, locally scoped, with a
unique answering section -- and the design position that the pipeline fails
closed: out-of-corpus queries are rejected on a distance threshold before
generation, trading recall for precision on the argument that an unsupported
answer costs more than a refusal.

======================================================================
CHUNKING STRATEGY
======================================================================

Chunk size: 800 characters, as a packing ceiling rather than a target width
Overlap:    none -- superseded by a propagated title and section header

Argument recorded: this corpus carries explicit structural markup that already
encodes its semantic boundaries. Each guide is partitioned under ## headings
into topically independent sections; the length distribution is tight, with a
maximum document of 2,547 characters and a maximum section near 600. Fixed-
width windowing therefore imposes an arbitrary partition over text that
already has a principled one.

The 800-character bound is retained as a guard for corpora with different
length characteristics. It does not fire on city_guides, so the mapping is
effectively one chunk per section; where it does fire, the split is taken at a
sentence boundary.

Overlap was eliminated rather than tuned. Its failure mode -- an answer lost
across a boundary -- presupposes boundaries placed without regard to content.
The residual risk once boundaries follow sections is different in kind: a
retrieved chunk that cannot identify its own subject, since "## Where to stay"
is near-identical across all ten town guides in isolation. A propagated header
addresses that at roughly 30 characters of overhead against the 120 the
overlap consumed.

One revision is documented: the boundary predicate initially matched markdown
## only, which degrades silently on plain-text corpora. Validation against
practice surfaced chunks terminating on an orphaned heading line, and the
predicate was generalised to admit short unpunctuated single-line paragraphs.

======================================================================
HOW I USED AI
======================================================================

Two interactions, each recorded as request / response / intervention.

1. Implementing the boundary-aware chunker. The returned design was sound --
   paragraph packing with heading-triggered flushes -- but the initial
   implementation carried two defects observable only in its output: chunks
   containing a heading and no body, and loss of the heading on the chunk
   following a flush, leaving section continuations unidentifiable. Common
   root cause: the buffer was flushed on encountering a heading without
   testing whether it yet held body content. A third defect surfaced under
   validation against a corpus it was not developed on (practice, plain-text
   headings).

   The transferable observation, recorded in the README: the failure surface
   was the data, not the control flow. Each defect was invisible under code
   review and immediate on inspection of emitted chunks, which argues for
   treating output inspection as the primary verification step for this class
   of component.

2. Specifying acceptance criteria 4 and 5. The substantive intervention was a
   factual error in the draft -- chunk count asserted as 92 against an actual
   88 documents -- detected on verification against the filesystem. Recorded
   because criterion 4 derives its value from being mechanically checkable: a
   criterion parameterised by a wrong constant fails for reasons orthogonal to
   pipeline behaviour, which is the exact failure mode acceptance criteria
   exist to exclude. The same pass surfaced a corpus inconsistency across
   config.py, questions.py and the Milestone 3 output.

======================================================================
CRITERIA CORRECTED
======================================================================

Criteria 4 and 5 were drafted while the project still pointed at campus_life
and did not survive the switch to city_guides. Criterion 4 asserted "exactly
88 chunks, one per document", which is a true structural consequence for
campus_life (88 short documents, none reaching the size bound) and false for
city_guides (14 documents, 96 chunks, splitting by design). A criterion that
is false by construction measures nothing.

Restated against the current corpus and verified:

    chunks 96 | mid-sentence 0 | no title 0

Criterion 4 now asserts section-granular self-containment -- no chunk
terminating mid-sentence, and every chunk carrying the title identifying its
subject -- both holding for 96 of 96. Criterion 5 is unchanged in substance
but re-argued from this corpus: attribution can be wrong while looking right,
because all ten town guides share a section schema.

Reasons were also written for criteria 1 to 3, which had targets but no
justification. Criterion 1 predicts in advance which query will miss and why
(Kestrelford Sunday transport, answering chunk at rank 10, outranked by
sections with higher lexical overlap). Criterion 3 records the measured class
separation and explains why 4 of 5 is deliberately slack given that the
negatives sample the easy tail.
